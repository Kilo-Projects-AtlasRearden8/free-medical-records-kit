**How to fill this out**
- Copy the name and strength straight from the label.
- "How often" means times per day and time of day (for example: "twice daily, morning and bedtime").
- Update this list whenever a medicine starts, stops, or changes dose. Cross out old rows instead of erasing — you may need the history later.
- Include inhalers, eye drops, creams, patches, and herbal supplements too.

**Questions to ask your pharmacist or provider**
