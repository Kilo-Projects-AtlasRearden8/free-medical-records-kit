**How to use this tracker**
- Ask each provider or pharmacy to document the vaccine, lot number, and date whenever you get a shot.
- Keep a photo or scan of each immunization card with this sheet.
- Bring this tracker to yearly checkups and before travel, new jobs, or school enrollment — some places require proof of vaccines.
- If you're unsure what you're due for, ask your provider. Schedules change with age, health conditions, and pregnancy.

**Common adult vaccines to ask about:** flu (yearly), COVID-19, Tdap/Td booster (every 10 years), shingles (age 50+), pneumococcal (age 65+, or younger with certain conditions).
