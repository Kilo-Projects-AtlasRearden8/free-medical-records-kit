# Request-Records Letters

Under HIPAA, you generally have the right to see and get copies of your own medical records. Use these fill-in-the-blank letters to request records from a provider, clinic, hospital, or pharmacy.

**Keep copies of everything you send**, and send requests by certified mail or through the provider's patient portal so you have proof of delivery. Providers may charge a reasonable copying fee and often have up to 30 days to respond.

## Letter 1: Request my complete medical records

[Your name]  
[Your street address]  
[City, State ZIP]  
[Phone number]  
[Email]  
[Date]

[Provider or facility name]  
Attn: Medical Records / Health Information Management  
[Street address]  
[City, State ZIP]

Re: Request for medical records  
Patient: [Your full name]  
Date of birth: [Your date of birth]  
Medical record number (if known): ___________________

To Whom It May Concern:

I am requesting a complete copy of my medical records, including office visit notes, test results, imaging reports, immunization records, medication lists, and discharge summaries, from [date range, e.g., "January 1, 2020 to present"].

Please send the records to:

[Your name]  
[Your street address]  
[City, State ZIP]

or through my patient portal account: [portal email or username]

If you require a signed authorization form, please let me know and I will complete and return it promptly. Please contact me at [phone number] with any questions or if there are fees associated with this request.

Thank you for your help.

Sincerely,

[Your signature]  
[Your printed name]

## Letter 2: Request records from a specific visit or date range

Same format as Letter 1, but replace the first paragraph with:

"I am requesting copies of my medical records related to treatment for [condition or reason] between [start date] and [end date], including [progress notes / lab results / imaging / operative reports / discharge summary]."

---

## Letter 3: Request records be sent to another provider

Same format as Letter 1, but replace the delivery paragraph with:

"Please send the records directly to my [new/specialist] provider:

[Provider name]  
[Street address]  
[City, State ZIP]  
Fax: [fax number]"

I authorize [original provider] to release my records to this provider for continuity of care.

[Your signature]  
[Your printed name]  
Date: ______________

---

## Tips for requesting records

- Include your full name, date of birth, and the dates of treatment so records are easy to locate.
- Ask for records in the format you want (paper or electronic). Electronic copies are often free or cheaper.
- If a provider denies your request, you can ask for the reason in writing and file a complaint with the U.S. Department of Health and Human Services Office for Civil Rights at hhs.gov/ocr.
- If you need records for someone else (a child, parent, or person you care for), you'll usually need proof of guardianship or a signed authorization from them.
