# Free Medical Records Kit

Fillable, printable worksheets for organizing your medical records: provider log, medication list, immunization tracker, appointment history, and records-request letters. No account needed. Nothing is stored online. Education only — not medical advice.

**Created by Asdale Dynamics, LLC** — Dylan Asdale, a registered nurse turned AI author and founder, based in Indiana. Asdale Dynamics publishes beginner-friendly AI education, books, and practical tools. This kit is one of our accessible life-admin helpers: paperwork made simpler, no jargon, no condescension.

## What's inside

| Form | What it's for |
|---|---|
| [`kit/provider-log.md`](kit/provider-log.md) | Doctors, specialists, pharmacies, dentists — contact info, roles, patient portal logins |
| [`kit/medication-list.md`](kit/medication-list.md) | Current medicines, doses, how often, prescriber, pharmacy, start/stop dates |
| [`kit/immunization-tracker.md`](kit/immunization-tracker.md) | Vaccines, dates, doses, reactions, next dose due |
| [`kit/appointment-history.md`](kit/appointment-history.md) | Visit dates, providers, reasons, follow-ups, referrals |
| [`kit/request-records-letters.md`](kit/request-records-letters.md) | Fill-in-the-blank letters to request your medical records from providers |
| [`dist/medical-records-kit.html`](dist/medical-records-kit.html) | All five forms as one fillable, printable web page (works offline) |

Each form is also available as plain-text Markdown in [`kit/`](kit/) — print it, copy it into a notes app, or adapt it to your needs.

## How to use it

You don't need to install anything or create an account. Everything stays on your device.

### HTML version (fill in your browser, then print)

1. **Open the kit.** Open [`dist/medical-records-kit.html`](dist/medical-records-kit.html) in your browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox — any modern browser works). You'll see a cover page followed by one page per form.
2. **Fill it in.** Type directly into the fields on screen. Field labels are paired with each input so the form works with screen readers. Nothing is uploaded, and no account is needed.
3. **Print or save as PDF.** Click **"Print / Save as PDF"** at the top of the page, or use your browser's print dialog (Ctrl+P / Cmd+P). Choose "Save as PDF" if you'd like a digital copy. When you print, the on-screen buttons disappear automatically, leaving just the clean worksheets.
4. **Start over anytime.** Click **Clear all fields** to blank the form, or refresh the page. Nothing leaves your device unless you print, save, or share it yourself.

### Markdown version (print from any Markdown app)

Open any file in [`kit/`](kit/) in a text editor, Markdown viewer, or notes app. Fill in the blanks, save, and print. The tables use empty rows so you can write in them by hand.
